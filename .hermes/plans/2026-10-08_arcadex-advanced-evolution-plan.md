## 🚀 Phase-by-Phase Implementation Roadmap

### 📦 PHASE 1: Multi-Core WebAssembly & Retro Emulation Pipeline
* **J2ME WASM Integration:** Embed a lightweight WebAssembly J2ME micro-runtime that executes `.jar` files in browser memory with realistic Nokia N-Gage and Sony Ericsson skins.
* **JS-DOS v86 WASM Engine:** Pre-configure open-source DOSBox WASM for public domain and freeware classics (Commander Keen, Doom Shareware, Prince of Persia).
* **Godot 4 & Unity WebGL Bridge:** Universal postMessage API bridge for indie developers to drop ZIP files containing Godot/Unity Web exports.

### 💰 PHASE 2: Advanced Monetization & AdSense for Games (AFG) Compliance
* **Google IMA SDK / AFG Integration:** Non-intrusive rewarded video & interstitial placement (e.g. "Continue Game?", "Unlock Cyber Skin?").
* **Layout Shift Protection (CLS = 0):** Pre-allocated aspect-ratio containers for banner slots ensuring Google AdSense approval without policy violations.
* **50/50 Developer Revenue Share Engine:** Client-side impression calculation and automated payout ledger for community submissions.

### 🌐 PHASE 3: Zero-Server P2P Multiplayer & Social Replay
* **WebRTC DataChannel Mesh:** Instant 2-player multiplayer for Pong, Chess, Tanks, and Racing via WebRTC direct browser-to-browser connection (0% server relay costs).
* **QR Code Room Invites:** Instant mobile-to-desktop play by scanning on-screen QR codes.
* **Canvas Clip Recorder:** Built-in `MediaRecorder` allowing players to clip 5-second highlights and export MP4/GIFs for social sharing.

### 🎨 PHASE 4: CrazyGames UX Perfection & Ambient Glow Canvas
* **Dynamic Ambient Lighting (Backlight):** Canvas color sampler that casts a dynamic ambient glow behind the game player matching real-time screen colors.
* **Cinema Mode & Aspect Ratio Lock:** Instant switching between 16:9 Desktop, 4:3 CRT Retro, and 9:16 Mobile Vertical formats.
* **Sound Synthesizer Customizer:** Built-in 8-bit chip synthesizer settings (Volume, Stereo Reverb, Low-Pass filter).

### 📈 PHASE 5: Advanced Programmatic SEO, AI Search Engine Optimization & PWA
* **Dynamic OG Social Cards:** Serverless canvas-generated preview cards with high-CTR badges when shared on Telegram/Discord/WhatsApp.
* **Multi-Language Localization (i18n):** Client-side translations for English, Spanish, French, German, Bengali, Arabic, and Hindi.
* **Full PWA Offline Support:** Service Worker offline caching so users can play games with no internet.
